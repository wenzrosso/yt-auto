yt-auto

yt-auto is a small personal automation tool. It writes short scripts, adds a synthetic voice-over and stock footage, assembles the video, and uploads it to the developer's own YouTube channel on a schedule.

It is not a public service, has no user accounts, and does not collect any information from visitors. The only Google permission it uses is the YouTube upload permission, granted by the developer for the developer's own channel.
